SalaryWise, developed by suryanshu15

How to request deletion

  1. Open the SalaryWise app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (from the dashboard menu).
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm by tapping Delete permanently.

Your account and data are deleted immediately — there is no waiting period. If you can't sign in to the app, email erudatasolutions@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it manually within 30 days.
